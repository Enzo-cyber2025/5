## How Rain Forms: A Detailed Explanation

Rain is a fascinating natural process that involves several key steps, primarily revolving around the **water cycle** and the processes of **condensation** and **precipitation**.

Here is a detailed explanation of how rain forms:

### 1. Evaporation (The Start)

The process begins with the sun's energy acting on bodies of water (oceans, lakes, rivers). Solar energy heats this water, causing the water molecules to gain energy and turn into a gas—water vapor. This process is called **evaporation**. The water vapor rises into the atmosphere.
